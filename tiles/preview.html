ChiefTiles

Letter tile design preview. Monoline SVG glyphs (no fonts), colours from the brand tokens, zero external requests.

Version · design proof of concept, not final.

Try a tile

One letter A-Z for a game tile (render() takes up to 3 characters, e.g. QU for a later die face) and a value 0-99. Size, stroke and back label come from the bar above.

Rack on a phone

Seven tiles at 44 px, roughly what a rack looks like on an iPhone, next to an opponent's rack: seven backs at 44 px.

Alphabet A-Z

Every glyph once, placeholder values (not a distribution).

Digits 0-9

The value digits as tile letters (render() accepts digits; a set tile is always one letter A-Z).

Blanks and backs

Blank (empty and playing E), the back without a label and with ChiefWords, ChiefGrid and the label field.

Letter sets

Real distributions from our own word lists (sets/nl.json, sets/en.json: 100 tiles for the large board; nl_small, en_small: 60 tiles for the small board) plus the demo. Every letter once with its count, plus the blanks.

Boards

Empty boards from boards/*.json (renderBoard): large 15×15 with the 100-tile sets, small 11×11 with the 60-tile sets. The game draws the tiles on top. minimal: every square has a ±1 px border, cellStroke accent (default) or dim; classic ignores cellStroke.
